## Foundations & Concepts

- [Blockchain Explained](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/blockchain.asp) – High-level explanation of blockchain concepts and architecture.
- [Bitcoin Whitepaper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf) – Foundational paper introducing decentralized digital currency.
- [Ethereum Whitepaper](https://ethereum.org/en/whitepaper/) – Proposal for programmable smart contracts on blockchain.
- [Consensus Mechanisms](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/) – Overview of PoW, PoS, and alternative consensus models.
- [Cryptography Basics](https://cryptopals.com/) – Practical cryptography fundamentals relevant to blockchain systems.

## Layer 1 Blockchains

- [Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org/) – First decentralized blockchain focused on peer-to-peer digital money.
- [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/) – Smart contract platform powering decentralized applications.
- [Solana](https://solana.com/) – High-throughput blockchain optimized for performance.
- [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) – Multi-chain platform enabling interoperable blockchains.
- [Cardano](https://cardano.org/) – Research-driven blockchain focused on scalability and security.
- [Avalanche](https://www.avax.network/) – High-performance blockchain supporting custom networks.
- [Cosmos](https://cosmos.network/) – Ecosystem of interoperable blockchains using IBC.

## Layer 2 & Scaling

- [Lightning Network](https://lightning.network/) – Layer 2 payment network for Bitcoin.
- [Arbitrum](https://arbitrum.io/) – Ethereum optimistic rollup for scaling smart contracts.
- [Optimism](https://www.optimism.io/) – Ethereum Layer 2 focused on optimistic rollups.
- [zkSync](https://zksync.io/) – Zero-knowledge rollup for scalable Ethereum transactions.
- [StarkNet](https://starknet.io/) – ZK-rollup platform for scalable smart contracts.
- [Polygon](https://polygon.technology/) – Scaling and interoperability solutions for Ethereum.

## Smart Contract Platforms

- [Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM)](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/evm/) – Runtime environment for Ethereum smart contracts.
- [Solana Programs](https://docs.solana.com/developing/programming-model/overview) – Smart contract model for Solana.
- [NEAR Protocol](https://near.org/) – Developer-friendly smart contract blockchain.
- [Tezos](https://tezos.com/) – Self-amending blockchain with on-chain governance.
- [Algorand](https://www.algorand.com/) – Blockchain optimized for speed and finality.

## Development Frameworks & SDKs

- [Hardhat](https://hardhat.org/) – Ethereum development environment for smart contracts.
- [Foundry](https://book.getfoundry.sh/) – Fast, Rust-based Ethereum development toolkit.
- [Truffle](https://trufflesuite.com/) – Smart contract development framework.
- [Brownie](https://eth-brownie.readthedocs.io/) – Python-based Ethereum development framework.
- [Web3.js](https://web3js.readthedocs.io/) – JavaScript library for interacting with blockchains.
- [Ethers.js](https://docs.ethers.org/) – Lightweight JavaScript library for Ethereum interactions.

## Nodes, Clients & Infrastructure

- [Geth](https://geth.ethereum.org/) – Official Go Ethereum client.
- [Nethermind](https://nethermind.io/) – High-performance Ethereum execution client.
- [Erigon](https://github.com/ledgerwatch/erigon) – Optimized Ethereum client for fast sync.
- [Infura](https://www.infura.io/) – Managed blockchain node infrastructure.
- [Alchemy](https://www.alchemy.com/) – Blockchain developer platform and APIs.
- [QuickNode](https://www.quicknode.com/) – High-performance blockchain node provider.
- [LYFTIUM](https://www.lyftium.com/) – Ethereum Mainnet JSON-RPC (rpm pricing, `X-Api-Key`, fail-closed 503).

## Wallets & Key Management

- [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/) – Browser-based wallet for Ethereum and EVM chains.
- [Ledger](https://www.ledger.com/) – Hardware wallet for secure key storage.
- [Trezor](https://trezor.io/) – Open-source hardware wallet.
- [WalletConnect](https://walletconnect.com/) – Protocol for connecting wallets to dApps.
- [Safe (Gnosis Safe)](https://safe.global/) – Multi-signature wallet for smart contract assets.

## DeFi (Decentralized Finance)

- [Uniswap](https://uniswap.org/) – Decentralized exchange using automated market makers.
- [Aave](https://aave.com/) – DeFi lending and borrowing protocol.
- [Compound](https://compound.finance/) – Algorithmic interest rate markets.
- [MakerDAO](https://makerdao.com/) – Decentralized stablecoin and lending system.
- [Curve](https://curve.fi/) – DEX optimized for stablecoin trading.

## NFTs & Digital Assets

- [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/) – Marketplace for NFTs and digital collectibles.
- [ERC-721](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721) – Standard for non-fungible tokens on Ethereum.
- [ERC-1155](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155) – Multi-token standard for NFTs and fungible tokens.
- [IPFS](https://ipfs.tech/) – Distributed storage commonly used for NFT metadata.
- [Arweave](https://www.arweave.org/) – Permanent decentralized data storage network.

## Interoperability & Oracles

- [Chainlink](https://chain.link/) – Decentralized oracle network for off-chain data.
- [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/) – Omnichain interoperability protocol.
- [Wormhole](https://wormhole.com/) – Cross-chain messaging protocol.
- [IBC](https://ibcprotocol.org/) – Inter-Blockchain Communication protocol in Cosmos.
- [Axelar](https://axelar.network/) – Cross-chain communication network.

## Security, Auditing & Privacy

- [OpenZeppelin](https://www.openzeppelin.com/) – Security standards and audited smart contract libraries.
- [MythX](https://mythx.io/) – Smart contract security analysis platform.
- [Slither](https://github.com/crytic/slither) – Static analysis framework for Solidity.
- [CertiK](https://www.certik.com/) – Blockchain security audits and monitoring.
- [Tornado Cash](https://tornado.cash/) – Privacy-preserving transaction protocol.

## Data, Analytics & Indexing

- [The Graph](https://thegraph.com/) – Decentralized indexing protocol for blockchain data.
- [Dune](https://dune.com/) – SQL-based blockchain analytics platform.
- [Glassnode](https://glassnode.com/) – On-chain market intelligence and analytics.
- [Nansen](https://www.nansen.ai/) – Blockchain analytics and wallet intelligence.
- [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/) – Ethereum blockchain explorer.

## Governance & DAOs

- [DAOstack](https://daostack.io/) – Framework for decentralized governance.
- [Aragon](https://aragon.org/) – Tools for creating and managing DAOs.
- [Snapshot](https://snapshot.org/) – Off-chain voting platform for DAOs.
- [Tally](https://www.tally.xyz/) – Governance dashboard for on-chain voting.
- [OpenZeppelin Governor](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/governance) – Governance contracts for DAOs.

## Enterprise & Consortium Blockchain

- [Hyperledger Fabric](https://www.hyperledger.org/use/fabric) – Permissioned blockchain framework for enterprises.
- [Hyperledger Besu](https://www.hyperledger.org/use/besu) – Enterprise-grade Ethereum client.
- [Corda](https://www.r3.com/corda-platform/) – Distributed ledger platform for regulated industries.
- [Quorum](https://consensys.net/quorum/) – Enterprise-focused Ethereum distribution.
- [IBM Blockchain](https://www.ibm.com/blockchain) – Enterprise blockchain solutions.
